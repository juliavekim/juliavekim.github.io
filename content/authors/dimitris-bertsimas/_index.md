Dimitris Bertsimas is the Boeing Leaders for Global Operations Professor of Management and Professor of Operations Research at MIT, and Vice Provost for Open Learning. His research includes optimisation, stochastic systems, and machine learning, with applications in areas such as healthcare, transportation, and finance.

I am working with Professor Bertsimas and Romain Puech on an **offline generative AI architecture for assessment-driven remediation videos**. The project explores reusable instructional components and precomputed personalised videos; learning effectiveness has not yet been evaluated.
