Romain Puech is a PhD student at MIT’s Operations Research Center, advised by Dimitris Bertsimas. His research combines machine learning and optimisation, with a focus on generative AI for education. At MIT Open Learning, he works on AI tutoring, adaptive teaching, and agentic systems that support instructors and learners.

Romain is a coauthor of **An Offline Architecture for Assessment-Driven Remediation Videos**, with Dimitris Bertsimas and Julia Kim.
