Risk-adaptive multimodal assistance for blind and low-vision users.

Trained a **CLIP urgency classifier** achieving **0.905 test ROC-AUC**. Fine-tuned **Kimi-VL with LoRA**, reducing composite loss by **38%** versus the uniform-policy baseline on **1,283 test examples**. Compared SFT and DPO, examining preference-pair design and robustness to routing errors.

Joint project with Xander Backus. My work focused on the unified adapter, CLIP classifier, training and evaluation.
