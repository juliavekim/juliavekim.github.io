Julia Kim

PhD student @ MIT ORC

Hi, I’m Julia! I’m a second-year PhD student at the MIT Operations Research Center, advised by Professor John Horton. My research interests include multimodal AI, automated research, and AI for financial decision-making. I’m currently working with John Horton and The Carlyle Group on AI agents that reconstruct and evaluate investment decisions using the information available at the time.

Before MIT, I studied mathematics at Cambridge and physics, mathematics, and statistics at the University of Toronto. I’ve also worked in The Trade Desk’s AI Lab. You can find more information in my CV. I hope my next chapter takes me to France or Italy to work in AI/ML research and engineering.

Personalized Learning with Generative AI: A Framework for Assessment-Driven Remediation Videos

Julia Kim, Dimitris Bertsimas

We combine reusable remediation videos with personalised visual annotations. Missed questions select the lesson; incorrect answers and optional tutoring dialogue select clarifications shown during playback, without changing the narration or video.

PortIA: Language Models for Portfolio Optimization

Julia Kim, Lindsay Carlin, Jacob Dentes, Salvador Dzimah

PortIA translates natural-language investment preferences into portfolio constraints, then uses mathematical optimization to construct portfolios. The project combines language models, market data, and sparse portfolio selection.
