Multimodal AI / MIT course research project / 2026

EgoBlind-RA

Visual assistance that responds to urgency.

With Xander Backus, I investigated how an assistive vision-language model should adapt its responses to the safety stakes of a query. My work focused on the CLIP urgency classifier and a unified, prompt-conditioned Kimi-VL LoRA adapter.

Supervised fine-tuning reduced composite loss by 38% against the uniform-policy baseline on 1,283 test examples. Three DPO variants performed worse than supervised fine-tuning in the unified setting, motivating analysis of preference pairs and failure cases.

LLM applications / Learning systems

Personalized Instruction

Turning misconceptions into targeted learning.

A Python pipeline for MIT's AI for Leaders course: map lecture materials to learning objectives, generate misconception-focused questions, and compose remediation videos around students' incorrect answers.

Reusable instructional content, personalised diagnosis slides, generated visuals, text-to-speech, and FFmpeg assembly—with staged execution, logging, and cache invalidation.

Scientific machine learning / Cambridge thesis

StarCLIP

A shared language for stellar spectra.

Contrastive learning to align APOGEE and JWST/NIRSpec stellar spectra in a shared latent space, supporting similarity search and stellar-property estimation across modalities.

My Cambridge thesis connects deep learning with scientific inference under limited data and imperfect overlap between observations.
